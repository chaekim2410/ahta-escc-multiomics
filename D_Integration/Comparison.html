5.1. GOI

In this analysis, positive logFC values indicate up-regulated genes, meaning these genes have increased expression in the experimental condition compared to the control. Genes like CFLAR, KDM1A, and RBM6 are examples of up-regulated genes. On the other hand, negative logFC values indicate down-regulated genes, meaning these genes have decreased expression in the experimental condition. Examples of down-regulated genes include STPG1, SEMA3F, ANKIB1, HECW1, MAD1L1, and ICA1, which all show reduced expression. Therefore, genes with positive logFC values are up-regulated, while genes with negative logFC values are down-regulated in the experimental group.
